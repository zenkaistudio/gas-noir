PROCESS
Client Journey

Five Stages.
One Standard.

We've refined this process across every client engagement. It's built to eliminate confusion, reduce back-and-forth, and get you to results faster — without cutting corners at any stage.

01
Stage One

Discovery

Before we write a single word or shoot a single frame, we listen. We run a structured discovery call (30–60 minutes) to understand your brand, goals, audience, competitive landscape, and what success looks like to you. This is where we decide if we're the right fit — and where you decide the same about us.

02
Stage Two

Strategy

Once we're aligned, we build your strategy. Depending on the service, this could be a content strategy, a brand positioning framework, a revenue model, or a technical brief. Every strategy is bespoke — no templates, no copy-paste from another client. This is the foundation everything else is built on.

03
Stage Three

Build

This is where we execute. Content gets produced, websites get designed, automations get built, social calendars go live. You'll receive progress updates and check-ins at pre-agreed milestones — no black holes, no radio silence. You always know where we are.

04
Stage Four

Launch

We don't just deliver and disappear. Launch is a critical phase — we coordinate go-live, brief you on how to use what we've built, and ensure everything is performing as expected from day one. For campaigns, we're active on launch day to monitor performance and catch any issues in real time.

05
Stage Five

Optimize

The best results come from iteration, not perfection. After launch, we review performance data, gather feedback, and identify what to adjust, double down on, or cut. This is where month-one clients start compounding their results — and why many of our clients stay on retainer long after their initial project ends.
