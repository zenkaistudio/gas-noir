Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Gas Noir Digital ("we", "us", or "our") operates gasnoir.digital. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a contact form or inquiry
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or download a resource
  • Book a discovery call through our scheduling system
  • Engage with us on social media platforms

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any message content you include.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behaviour, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited. This information is collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested services
  • Send you the resources, newsletters, or updates you've opted into
  • Improve our website and service offerings
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you do not accept cookies, some portions of our site may not function properly.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services including Google Analytics, Calendly, and email marketing platforms. These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information. We encourage you to review their policies separately.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, subject to confidentiality agreements.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@gasnoir.digital.

GDPR & CCPA

If you are located in the European Union or California, you have specific rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively. These include the right to know what data we collect, the right to deletion, the right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell data), and the right to non-discrimination. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@gasnoir.digital or through our contact form.
